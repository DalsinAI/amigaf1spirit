F-1 Spirit for AmigaOS 3.x: what is in the package, and under which terms
========================================================================

The game
--------
F-1 Spirit, "the unofficial remake of Konami's F-1 SPIRIT" (MSX, 1987), by
Brain Games (Santi "Popolon" Ontanon and the others in ReadMe-f1spirit's
credits), (C) 2003-2009 Brain Games, release 0.rc9 build 1615, from
https://braingames.jorito.net/f1spirit/f1spirit.src_0.rc9-1615.tgz.

Its source archive carries one licence statement, in debian/copyright
(written by Patsie, Brain Games' packager, 2009): the game, by Brain Games,
under the "GPL", no version named. The archive has no COPYING file and its
files no notices of their own; the GNU GPL says a program that names no
version may be used under any version. COPYING.txt here is version 2,
the version this port's own files are under.

That statement covers the whole archive: the program and its data
(graphics/, sound/, tracks/, demos/). The package ships them as they are.

F-1 Spirit is a remake of a Konami game: its name, its look and its
tracks follow Konami's original, and readme.txt (ReadMe-f1spirit) says
Brain Games is not related to Konami and made it not for profit. This
port is likewise not for sale and not connected with Konami.

The port
--------
The port's own files (amiga/, and the changes marked __amigaos__ in
sources/) are by the AmiCradle Team, under the GPL, version 2 or later.

Built into the program:
- stb_image 2.30 and stb_vorbis 1.22 (Sean Barrett): public domain, or
  the MIT licence.
- GFOpenGPU's SDL 1.2 (sdl12-compat on SDL 2): the zlib licence; its
  OpenGL (Mesa): the MIT licence.
